FACTS ABOUT WOOL

SOURCE: SHEEP AND OTHER WOOL-PRODUCING ANIMALS

TYPE: NATURAL ANIMAL FIBER

BIODEGRADABLE: YES

COMMON USES: SWEATERS, COATS, SCARVES, SOCKS AND BLANKETS

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT: WOOL IS BIODEGRADABLE AND DURABLE, BUT SHEEP FARMING CAN REQUIRE LAND, WATER AND OTHER RESOURCES AND CAN PRODUCE GREENHOUSE GAS EMISSIONS.
