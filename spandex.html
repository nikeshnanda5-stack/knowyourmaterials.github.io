FACTS ABOUT SPANDEX

SOURCE: PETROLEUM-BASED CHEMICALS

TYPE: SYNTHETIC FIBER

BIODEGRADABLE: NO

COMMON USES: SPORTSWEAR, SWIMWEAR, UNDERWEAR, LEGGINGS AND STRETCH JEANS

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT: SPANDEX IS MADE FROM FOSSIL-FUEL-BASED CHEMICALS AND DOES NOT BIODEGRADE EASILY. IT CAN ALSO RELEASE MICROFIBERS DURING WASHING.
