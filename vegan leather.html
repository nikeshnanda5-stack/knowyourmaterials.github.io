FACTS ABOUT VEGAN LEATHER

SOURCE: SYNTHETIC OR PLANT-BASED MATERIALS

TYPE: ALTERNATIVE LEATHER MATERIAL

BIODEGRADABLE: DEPENDS ON THE MATERIAL

COMMON USES: JACKETS, SHOES, BAGS, BELTS AND WALLETS

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT: MANY VEGAN LEATHERS ARE MADE FROM PLASTIC-BASED MATERIALS SUCH AS PU OR PVC, WHICH CAN USE FOSSIL FUELS AND DO NOT BIODEGRADE EASILY. PLANT-BASED VERSIONS CAN HAVE DIFFERENT ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACTS.
