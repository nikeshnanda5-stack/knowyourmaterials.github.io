FACTS ABOUT LEATHER

SOURCE: ANIMAL HIDES AND SKINS

TYPE: NATURAL ANIMAL MATERIAL

BIODEGRADABLE: YES, BUT PROCESSING CAN AFFECT DECOMPOSITION

COMMON USES: JACKETS, SHOES, BAGS, BELTS AND WALLETS

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT: LEATHER PRODUCTION USES ANIMAL HIDES AND CAN INVOLVE SIGNIFICANT WATER, ENERGY AND CHEMICAL USE DURING TANNING AND PROCESSING.
