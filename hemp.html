FACTS ABOUT HEMP

SOURCE: HEMP PLANT

TYPE: NATURAL PLANT FIBER

BIODEGRADABLE: YES

COMMON USES: SHIRTS, TROUSERS, JACKETS, DRESSES AND BAGS

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT: HEMP GENERALLY REQUIRES LESS WATER AND FEWER PESTICIDES THAN COTTON, BUT ITS ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT STILL DEPENDS ON HOW IT IS GROWN AND PROCESSED.
