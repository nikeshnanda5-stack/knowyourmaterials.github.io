FACTS ABOUT FLEECE

SOURCE: MOSTLY POLYESTER

TYPE: SYNTHETIC FABRIC

BIODEGRADABLE: NO

COMMON USES: JACKETS, HOODIES, SWEATSHIRTS, BLANKETS AND TRACKSUITS

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT: FLEECE IS OFTEN MADE FROM POLYESTER, A FOSSIL-FUEL-BASED MATERIAL THAT DOES NOT BIODEGRADE EASILY. IT CAN ALSO RELEASE MICROFIBERS DURING WASHING.
