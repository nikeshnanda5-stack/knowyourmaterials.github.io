GIVE CLOTHES A SECOND LIFE

Don't throw away clothes you no longer use. Choose an option below to give them a second life.

DONATE

Have clothes in good condition that you no longer need? Donate them to someone who can use them.

RESELL

If your clothes are still usable, you can resell them instead of throwing them away.

REPAIR

Damaged clothes don't always need to be thrown away. Repairing them can extend their useful life.

RECYCLE

If clothing can no longer be worn or repaired, consider recycling it through a suitable textile recycling program.
