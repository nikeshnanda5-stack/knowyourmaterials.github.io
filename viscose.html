FACTS ABOUT VISCOSE

SOURCE: WOOD PULP/PLANT CELLULOSE

TYPE: REGENERATED CELLULOSE FIBER

BIODEGRADABLE: YES

COMMON USES: DRESSES, SHIRTS, BLOUSES, LININGS AND HOME TEXTILES

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT: Viscose is made from plant cellulose, but its production can involve significant chemical use and can contribute to deforestation if the wood source is not responsibly managed.
