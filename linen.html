FACTS ABOUT LINEN

SOURCE: FLAX PLANT

TYPE: NATURAL FIBER

BIODEGRADABLE: YES

COMMON USES: SHIRTS, TROUSERS, DRESSES, BEDDING AND TABLECLOTHS

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT: LINEN IS MADE FROM FLAX, A PLANT THAT GENERALLY REQUIRES LESS WATER AND FEWER PESTICIDES THAN COTTON, BUT ITS ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT STILL DEPENDS ON HOW IT IS GROWN AND PROCESSED.
